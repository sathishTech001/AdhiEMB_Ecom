# 00.2 Business Requirements — AdhiEMB Enterprise Guide

## Functional Requirements
- **Internal Users**: Owner, Admin, Employee. Must have access to management portals.
- **External Users**: Designer, Customer. Customers browse, purchase, and stream files. Designers submit products.
- **Product Workflow**: DRAFT -> PENDING_APPROVAL -> APPROVED / REJECTED (+Reason).
- **Download Protection**: Every download requires a cryptographic token (`tok_...`). IDOR ownership validation enforced.
